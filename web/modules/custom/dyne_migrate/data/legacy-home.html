expanding what’s possible for people living with genetically driven neuromuscular diseases

We’re on a mission to deliver functional improvement for individuals, families and communities living with neuromuscular diseases. Functional improvement can mean achieving simple but meaningful activities like zipping a jacket, climbing up stairs or completing a morning routine. Dyne was built with one purpose: To help make these moments possible for the people who need them most. With this clear goal in mind, we work every day to deliver potentially life-changing therapies.
