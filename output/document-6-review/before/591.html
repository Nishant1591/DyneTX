What I find most rewarding about a career in science is that it allows me to actively contribute to solving real-world problems. At Dyne, I’m part of a groundbreaking team finding solutions to rare neuromuscular diseases. I’m proud to utilize my skills here as I nurture my love for continuous learning and intellectual growth.

Lydia Schlaefke

Working in the lab puts me at the front lines of discovery. The research we do at Dyne is especially fulfilling because I know it is always driven by a commitment to patients. Each day I go into work, I contribute to the development of potentially life-changing therapeutics. Knowing that my work has the power to make a real difference in people’s lives makes my career in science incredibly rewarding!

I love the continuous learning, the search for answers and most importantly the ability to answer challenging questions that can help patients and their families.

Reshmii Venkatesan

To me a career in science is fueled with curiosity leading to innovative discoveries. The most rewarding part is knowing that the research we carry out at Dyne can change lives of people living with neuromuscular diseases by giving advanced treatment that can turn their future filled with possibility.

Michelle Hammerschmidt

I am proud to be an Asian American woman in science, using my unique perspective to bring diverse and innovative solutions to clinical trial design. I strive to amplify the voices of patients through data-driven decision-making, particularly in the neuromuscular disease space. While there is still much work to be done by and for women in STEM, I embrace the responsibility and am honored to contribute to that progress.

Why we joined

What drew me to Dyne was the company’s commitment to developing revolutionary therapies that target muscle tissue with unprecedented precision. It’s a joy to lead a research team with such unwavering dedication to transforming the lives of people and families living with serious muscle diseases.

Oxana Beskrovnaya

Dyne’s approach has great potential to address the underlying cause of many different neuromuscular diseases, and to develop transformative therapies that uniquely draw from the experience of people living with those diseases. I’m delighted to be part of the team furthering that important mission.

Deb Feldman

I was interested in joining a company where the developed therapy doesn’t improve the quality of life incrementally but is truly life-changing for the patient – a therapy that targets the root cause of the disease, not just the symptoms. I wanted to work with a team that are experts in the field, dynamic, and patient-focused.

Tim Weeden

Dyne is an opportunity to take cutting-edge nucleic acid-based therapeutics and use our understanding of human biology to overcome the next set of hurdles in precision medicine. The team being built at Dyne is perfectly positioned to develop novel therapeutics for rare diseases, and I am ecstatic to have the opportunity to be a part of it.

Cody Desjardins

Notice on fraudulent job offers and recruitment scams

At Dyne Therapeutics, we are aware of fraudulent recruitment activities by individuals falsely claiming to represent our company. These scams often involve fake job offers requesting personal information or payment in exchange for employment.

Please note that Dyne will never request personal financial details, passwords, or payment as part of the hiring process. Legitimate emails from our recruitment team will only come from an official "@dyne-tx.com" email address.

If you receive an unexpected job offer or suspicious recruitment inquiry, we recommend not engaging, not clicking on any links, and not opening any attachments.

If you believe you have been targeted by a recruitment scam, please report it to your local law enforcement.
