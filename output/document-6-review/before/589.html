advancing targeted therapies designed to deliver functional improvement

We are on a mission to deliver functional improvement for people living with genetically driven neuromuscular diseases through targeted therapies enabled by our FORCE™ platform. We have assembled an exceptional team with deep experience in neuromuscular disease, rare disorders, drug development, and manufacturing and commercialization of novel therapeutics. We have also established a scientific advisory board comprised of leading experts in neuromuscular disease drug discovery and development.

Our leadership team
